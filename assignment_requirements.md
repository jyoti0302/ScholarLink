# Assignment Requirements Checklist

Everything requested in the attached PDF, organized for tracking and submission.

---

## 1. Problem Statement
- Pick a **real problem** you have seen up close and design a **digital app** to solve it.

## 2. Context & Problem Selection Criteria
- Choose a problem you have **personally experienced**, or watched someone you actually know (**friend, family member, hostel-mate, local shopkeeper, colleague**) struggle with repeatedly.
- **Avoid** problems based on trends or that you only know about from articles or social media.

## 3. Your Task (What to Demonstrate)
Show how you think by clearly addressing:
- [ ] **Who** has this problem.
- [ ] **Why** it matters.
- [ ] **Why an app** is the right way to solve it.
- [ ] **Your assumptions** (stated explicitly).
- [ ] **Verified vs. guessed:** Clearly distinguish what you verified (even a 10-minute conversation counts) versus what you are guessing.

---

## 4. Required Artifacts to Submit

### Artifact 1: 1-Page Write-Up (~300 to 700 words)
- [ ] Written **in your own words**.
- [ ] Length: approximately **300 to 700 words** (1 page).
- [ ] **a.** Your take on the problem statement.
- [ ] **b.** What is **in scope**.
- [ ] **c.** What is **out of scope**.
- [ ] **d.** What is **your solution** to this problem and **why**.
- [ ] **e.** **Ensure this page is NOT created by AI.**

### Artifact 2: Prompts Used
- [ ] Submit **the prompts you used** to create the tool.

### Artifact 3: Evals Used
- [ ] Submit **the evals you used** to test your solution.

### Artifact 4: App Link
- [ ] Submit a **link to the app** built on **Claude, Lovable, Bolt, or any other tool of your choice**.

---

## 5. Important Notes (Please Note)
- This assignment is only **one of the many inputs** used to evaluate candidates.
- You will **not** be evaluated on your domain knowledge.
- [ ] **Access check:** Ensure the evaluators **have access to the tool** (verify sharing/public permissions before submitting).
